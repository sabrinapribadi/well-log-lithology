# Table, chart or neither

| The reader must... | Use | Command |
|---|---|---|
| See a well's rock by depth and where to review | **Strip log**: rock column (conventional lithology colours), probability track, doubtful intervals hatched | `chart column WELL` |
| Judge the Kansas transfer | **Bars of median agreement** against the 50% pass line, lift and verdict printed | `chart units` |
| Read one Kansas well's log quality | **No chart**: numbered points | `quality KGS_ID` |
| Exact values | **Table** (each chart has a table view) | — |

Never: a chart in units the reader does not use, two y-axes, or a rock column without its legend.
